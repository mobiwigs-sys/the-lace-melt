How to Choose the Right Wig (Buying Guide)

1. HD vs. Transparent Lace

HD lace disappears on brown and dark skin without heavy makeup. Transparent lace works well with lace tint spray.

2. Density (150% vs 180%)

150% density gives a realistic everyday look. Choose 180%–200% for big curls or 22+ inch lengths.

3. 5x5 Closure vs. 13x4 Frontal

5x5 closures are glueless for beginners. 13x4 frontals allow half-up styles and deep side parts.

4. Cap Security

Always choose wigs with a built-in wide elastic band and breathable mesh cap for glue-free hold.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sleep in a glueless lace wig?

Remove glueless wigs before bed and place them on a mannequin head to prevent tangling and keep the lace flat.

How do I melt lace without glue?

Wrap an elastic melt band around your hairline for 10 minutes after misting an alcohol-free holding spray.

How do I stop curly wigs from frizzing?

Mist with water and lightweight leave-in conditioner, detangle gently while wet, and let curls air-dry.
