How to Choose the Right Wig (Buying Guide)

Before buying your next unit on Amazon, check these 4 essentials:

1. HD Lace vs. Transparent Lace

HD lace is ultra-thin and invisible on brown and dark skin tones without heavy foundation. Transparent lace works best with a quick spritz of lace tint.

2. Density (150% vs 180%)

150% density gives a realistic everyday look. Choose 180% to 200% for big curly wigs or long lengths (22+ inches).

3. 5x5 Closure vs. 13x4 Frontal

5x5 closures are glueless and best for beginners. 13x4 frontals let you create sleek half-up styles and deep side parts.

4. Cap Security

Always choose wigs with a built-in wide elastic band and breathable mesh cap so the wig stays snug without glue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sleep in a glueless lace wig?

It's best to remove glueless wigs before sleeping to prevent tangling and friction. Placing the unit on a mannequin head overnight keeps the lace flat and extends its lifespan.

How do I melt lace without glue?

Wrap an elastic melt band around your hairline for 10 minutes after applying a light mist of alcohol-free holding spray.

How do I stop curly wigs from frizzing?

Use a spray bottle with water and a lightweight leave-in conditioner, detangle gently with a wide-tooth comb while wet, and let the curls air-dry.
